      About this resource

      This page was made because the information a laboratory runs on tends to sit in places nobody can reach when it is needed — a manual in a drawer, a method in somebody's notebook, a hazard warning on a bottle at the back of a cupboard. It gathers that material in one place, on a phone, behind a QR code on the wall.

      It holds standard operating procedures for the department's instruments, the chemical inventory with storage and hazard information, laboratory protocols, media and buffer recipes, calculators, a safety section and a troubleshooting guide, together with a question bank for examination preparation.

      The content was compiled from manufacturers' manuals, standard textbooks and departmental practice, with AI tools used for drafting and formatting, and was reviewed before publication. It supplements, and does not replace, those original sources.

      It is prepared and maintained by Suraj Kumar Thakor, PhD Scholar in the Department of Plant Pathology, who is responsible for its content and keeps it updated. Corrections, additions and suggestions are genuinely welcome — if a step is wrong, a method is missing, or an instrument has been replaced, please write to surajthakor171119999@gmail.com or call +91 63552 16903, and it will be put right.
